1. IMPORTANT DATA MODEL DECISION
==========================================================

Do NOT create completely separate duplicated company records for customers and suppliers.

Create a shared business entity concept.

Use a model conceptually similar to:

BusinessParty

A BusinessParty represents a person or organization we do business with.

A party can have one or multiple roles:

CUSTOMER
SUPPLIER

Future roles may include:

SUBCONTRACTOR
PARTNER
SERVICE_PROVIDER
LANDLORD
OTHER

Do not implement future workflows now.

Example:

ABC Logistics may simultaneously be:

Customer = YES
Supplier = YES

There should still be ONE master BusinessParty record.

This is important for the future interconnected KHANBAS NEXUS platform.
